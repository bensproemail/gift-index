# 0002 Seven gift families, one per circuit group, scored as "top X%"

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11

## Context
Ben chose the full set of gift types with equal weight (option 2c, Nov 11 2026), all the way down to Color, Tone
and Base, with Ra's teaching first, later teachers labeled, and Gene Keys as its own labeled layer.

## Decision
- One family per circuit structure, so every one of the 36 channels belongs to exactly one family:
  Spiritual = Individual/Centering, Transformational = Individual/Knowing, Entrepreneurial = Tribal/Ego,
  Mastery = Collective/Logic, Storyteller = Collective/Sensing, Caretaker = Tribal/Defense,
  Presence = Individual/Integration. The family names are our labels, not Ra's.
- Raw score: 2 per defined channel, 1 per half channel. Reported as "top X%" of living people, which puts
  families of different size on one scale. Lead gift = smallest "top X%".
- Rarity and gifts are never blended into one number. Every gift is shown with its downside and source label.
- Gene Keys Gift words attach to gates as a separate labeled layer and never change a score.

## Consequences
Type, lines, centers, exalted/detriment planets and BG5 are not scored in v1. Adding them needs a new decision.
